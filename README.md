# Kasper Bergholt

I work with old cameras, old languages, and old texts: photography and what comes after it, system poetry, programs written for hardware that no longer ships.

## Works

### Sun: A Triptych

**[triptych](https://github.com/bergholt/triptych)** · [![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.21908151.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21908151)

A system poem in Prolog. Ten words and two syntactic orders give seventy-two variants, one for each eclipse of Saros 126, a cycle running from 1179 to 2459. The eclipse of 2026 is the forty-eighth. The program makes the poem’s constraints explicit and generates every combination they permit.

[About the work](https://bergholt.net/sun)

### Flora Excursoria Hafniensis

**[flora-excursoria-hafniensis](https://github.com/bergholt/flora-excursoria-hafniensis)** · [![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.21906483.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21906483)

A transcription of the 903-name index to Drejer’s 1838 flora of Copenhagen, read with Prolog. The index interfiles Latin and Danish names in one alphabet and offers no translations. A plant’s two names were set on the same page, however, so the page number works as a join key and part of the pairing can be recovered.

[About the work](https://bergholt.net/flora)

### EIN. MEER. VON. SAND.

**[sand](https://github.com/bergholt/sand)**

An audiovisual work for MS-DOS in Pascal and APL. The Danish and German inscriptions of the Sand Drift Monument at Tisvilde, erected in 1738, are read alongside English translations. Each letter selects a harmonic of A2 through a modulus that moves from twelve to seventeen. Characters leave the text and settle into a sea below it, while their earlier tunings remain in the drone.

The repository brings together the Pascal and APL source, DOS executable, soundtrack, ANSI artwork and DOSBox configuration, alongside notes on the work’s structure and preservation.

[About the work](https://bergholt.net/sand)
