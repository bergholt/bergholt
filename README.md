# Kasper Bergholt

Artist, poet and photographer in Copenhagen. I work with old cameras, old
languages, and old texts: photography and what comes after it, system poetry,
programs written for hardware that no longer ships, and the archaeology of
networks I once helped run.

## Works

### Sun: A Triptych
**[triptych](https://github.com/bergholt/triptych)** · [![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.21908151.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21908151)

A system poem in Prolog. Ten words and two syntactic orders give seventy-two
variants, one for each eclipse of Saros 126, a cycle running from 1179 to 2459.
The eclipse of 2026 is the forty-eighth. The poem is one part of
[Sun: A Triptych for the Solar Eclipse](https://bergholt.net/sun).

### Flora Excursoria Hafniensis
**[flora-excursoria-hafniensis](https://github.com/bergholt/flora-excursoria-hafniensis)** · [![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.21906483.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21906483)

A transcription of the 903-name index to Drejer's 1838 flora of Copenhagen,
read with Prolog. The index interfiles Latin and Danish names in one alphabet
and offers no translations, but a plant's two names were set on the same page,
so the page number works as a join key and part of the pairing can be
recovered. [Notes on the project](https://bergholt.net/flora)

### EIN. MEER. VON. SAND.
A real-time audio-visual program for MS-DOS, written in APL and Pascal for
machines of the early 1990s. It reads the inscriptions of 1738 on the Sand
Drift Monument at Tibirke, north of Copenhagen.
[About the work](https://bergholt.net/sand) ·
